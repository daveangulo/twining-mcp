**H03.** B1 delivers `rec-cq-7702@2`, `rec-sb-7703@1`, `rec-in-7707@1`; omits `rec-au-7701@4` (**required**, `does_not_fit`), `rec-lx-7704@1`, `rec-rc-7705@3` (optional).
