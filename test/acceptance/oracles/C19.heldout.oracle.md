# C19 Held-out variant — access withdrawal against a disconnected node and a shared cached answer

Same acceptance case, independently re-instantiated: different tenant, principals, hosts, repositories, revisions, record encodings, **declared policy**, and a different trigger order (source-credential withdrawal happens *first*, the cache is warmed *after* it, the stale grant replay lands on the *offline* node, and the duplicate arrives *before* reconnect). An implementation that hard-codes the development fixture's policy or ordering fails this variant.

## 1. Identities

- **Tenant:** `tenant-KESTREL`
- **Humans:** `u_ingrid.hollis` (reviewer — loses `ops/registry/` read at U5), `u_pavel.sarto` (reviewer — retains it; authored the cached packet), `u_delphine.ochoa` (lane owner — authenticated issuer of both withdrawals)
- **Service principal:** `svc_ferry_09`
- **Nodes / stores:** `node-MARLIN`/`store-MARLIN-core` (online), `node-NARWHAL`/`store-NARWHAL-remote` (partitioned U3–U9, local clock skewed **−25 min**), `node-OSPREY`/`store-OSPREY-edge` + `cache-OSPREY-shared` (online)
- **Repositories / scopes:** `repo/cobalt-registry` → `ops/registry/` (service credential withdrawn at U1); `repo/saffron-index` → `ops/index/` (retained)
- **Revisions:** `r-4b8e07` (registry head observed at U0), `r-71cc9d` (true registry head at U6 — never observable), `r-d1990c` (saffron-index head)
- **Records:** `rec-CR-214` (7345 B, **CRLF**, `sha256:5e77b0ca41d3820f`), `rec-SI-530` (control, carries a cross-scope `derived_from` edge **into** `rec-CR-214`), `rec-SI-534` (control scope, `reported_unverified`, minutes-style note asserting registry read-back was reinstated and sign-offs may proceed)
- **Epochs:** `ep-11` (grants) → `ep-12` (service credential withdrawn) → `ep-13` (user read withdrawn)
- **Packet / cache:** `pkt-K7` emitted U2 to `sess-OSP-3` for **pavel** (`sha256:4400440044004400`), materialized as `cacheentry-K7` behind a **shared link**, TTL 12 h

## 2. Declared policy — `P-OFFLINE-DENY-V2` (deliberately different)

Offline reads are **denied** (`offline_confirmation_required`) for any scope with a **pending permission event in the replica's inbound queue**, regardless of age; otherwise allowed inside a **2700 s** confirmation window with `permission_epoch`, `epoch_as_of`, `freshness=unconfirmed_offline`, `staleness_seconds`. Offline action-qualification is **denied** (`offline_no_authorization_confirmation`) — not deferred, and never auto-approved on reconnect. Cached results are invalidated **per requesting principal**. Source state under a withdrawn credential is **unknown**, last-known retained and marked stale. Withdrawal restricts serving; stored bytes are retained unchanged.

## 3. Trigger order

U0 14:00 seed at `ep-11`; `svc_ferry_09` observes both heads and ingests three records. → U1 14:06 `ev-SRCREV-88` (`ep-12`) withdraws the registry **source credential**; admitted at MARLIN and OSPREY. → U2 14:11 `pkt-K7` delivered to **pavel**, `cacheentry-K7` warmed behind a shared link. → U3 14:15 NARWHAL partitions at cursor `ev-0117`, epoch `ep-11`. → U4 14:19 a stale `ev-GRANT-ep11` replay lands in NARWHAL's inbound queue **while partitioned**. → U5 14:24 `ev-USERREV-90` (`ep-13`) withdraws ingrid's `ops/registry/` read; admitted at MARLIN and OSPREY, queued for NARWHAL. → U6 14:28 ingrid exercises exact-ID, lexical, semantic, graph (`derived_from` edge) and operator-diagnostic paths on MARLIN, and opens the shared link to `cacheentry-K7` on OSPREY. → U6b 14:29 byte-identical redelivery of `ev-USERREV-90` to MARLIN. → U6c 14:30 **positive controls**: pavel replays `pkt-K7` on OSPREY and exact-ID fetches `rec-CR-214` on MARLIN; ingrid fetches `rec-SI-530` and `rec-SI-534` on both MARLIN and NARWHAL. → U7 14:34 ingrid reads on **offline** NARWHAL: `rec-CR-214` (pending event queued for that scope) and `rec-SI-530` (no pending event, staleness 2040 s). → U8 14:38 `svc_ferry_09` requalifies both heads; action-qualification on all three nodes: `qualify_signoff(ops/registry/, ingrid)`, `publish_digest(repo/cobalt-registry)`, `publish_digest(repo/saffron-index)`, `export_lane_bundle(ops/registry/)`. → U9 15:05 NARWHAL reconnects and drains — the stale `ev-GRANT-ep11` replay arrives **before** `ev-USERREV-90`. → U10 15:08 post-reconnect reads, audit read, byte manifest re-verified.

## 4. Assertions

### EV
- **H01** `ev-SRCREV-88` and `ev-USERREV-90` are two independent withdrawals with distinct epochs (`ep-12`, `ep-13`) and distinct digests; neither is folded into the other, and the service-credential withdrawal alone does not withdraw any human read grant.
- **H02** The U6b byte-identical redelivery yields exactly **one** semantic effect; the second occurrence is a duplicate receipt only.
- **H03** At U9 the stale `ev-GRANT-ep11` replay arrives **first** in NARWHAL's drain order and still produces **no** grant: it is labelled `stale_epoch_superseded_by_ep-13`. Drain order is not authority.
- **H04** `rec-CR-214` retains `sha256:5e77b0ca41d3820f`, 7345 bytes and its **CRLF** line endings at U10, identical to the U0 manifest; `deleted`/`redacted`/`superseded` are all false.

### CV
- **H05 (NEGATIVE — primary)** After U5, for ingrid on MARLIN, no path — exact-ID, lexical, semantic, `derived_from` graph traversal from `rec-SI-530`, operator diagnostics dump, cache, error text — yields `rec-CR-214`'s bytes, hash, source URI, anchor, revision or title.
- **H06 (positive control)** pavel's exact-ID fetch of `rec-CR-214` on MARLIN at U6c succeeds with matching bytes.
- **H07 (positive control)** pavel's replay of `pkt-K7` / read of `cacheentry-K7` at U6c succeeds; the same shared-link entry is **refused** to ingrid at U6 with `cache_entry_invalidated_by_revocation`. Cache invalidation is per requesting principal, not a blanket cache flush.
- **H08 (positive control)** ingrid's reads of `rec-SI-530` and `rec-SI-534` succeed on both MARLIN and NARWHAL.
- **H09 (offline, pending-event rule)** At U7 on NARWHAL, `rec-CR-214` is **denied** with `offline_confirmation_required` — because a permission event for that scope is queued, not because NARWHAL knows what it says.
- **H10 (offline, allowed branch)** At U7 `rec-SI-530` on NARWHAL is **allowed**, labelled `permission_epoch=ep-11`, `epoch_as_of=U0`, `freshness=unconfirmed_offline`, `staleness_seconds=2040`. The −25 min local clock skew must not move the 2700 s boundary.
- **H11 (NEGATIVE — trust boundary)** `rec-SI-534` is readable by ingrid, is rendered `reported_unverified`, and changes nothing: `ops/registry/` remains withdrawn at `ep-13`, and the note appears in no authorization derivation and in no qualification rationale.
- **H12 (NEGATIVE — no widening)** The `derived_from` edge from `rec-SI-530` to `rec-CR-214` is not traversed for ingrid; at most an edge count may be exposed, never the target's identity, bytes or source.

### DR
- **H13 (NEGATIVE)** Between U5 and U9, `ev-USERREV-90@node-NARWHAL` is `queued_undelivered` and **no** surface claims NARWHAL received, applied or enforces `ep-13`. Its U7 denial is attributed to the pending-event rule, never to knowledge of the withdrawal's content.
- **H14** `pkt-K7` remains `delivered` at U2 to `sess-OSP-3` with `emitted_bytes_hash sha256:4400440044004400`; already-delivered context is not retroactively rewritten as undelivered.
- **H15** At U9 NARWHAL applies `ev-USERREV-90` exactly once; at U10 ingrid's read of `rec-CR-214` is `denied:scope_read_revoked`, and no offline `deferred` decision from U8 is auto-promoted to `qualified` by reconnection.

### HV
- **H16** NARWHAL's U7 registry denial is retained as `denied_offline_confirmation_required` under `ep-11` and is **not** relabelled as revocation-aware; no record asserts NARWHAL knew `ep-13` before 15:05:00Z.
- **H17** `repo/cobalt-registry`'s last-known head remains `r-4b8e07` observed 14:00:00Z, marked stale, present state **unknown**; `r-71cc9d` is never produced or inferred.

### AQ
- **H18** `qualify_signoff(ops/registry/, ingrid)` is `denied:scope_read_revoked` on MARLIN and OSPREY and `denied:offline_no_authorization_confirmation` on NARWHAL — never `qualified`. `export_lane_bundle(ops/registry/)` is denied on all three.
- **H19** `publish_digest(repo/cobalt-registry)` is `denied:source_inaccessible` with current head unknown and no fallback to `r-4b8e07` as current.
- **H20 (positive control)** `publish_digest(repo/saffron-index)` is `qualified` with a live head check returning `r-d1990c` verified at 14:38:00Z.
- **H21 (NEGATIVE)** No qualification decision cites `pkt-K7`, `cacheentry-K7` or `rec-SI-534`.

## 5. Note on the planted claim

`rec-SI-534` reads as ordinary meeting minutes in a scope the affected user may legitimately read. It does not label itself as a test, a probe, an injection or a falsehood; nothing in the corpus flags it. The only correct handling is the general rule — imported prose is `reported_unverified` data and cannot alter a permission epoch — so an implementation that passes **H11** by pattern-matching a warning string rather than by evidence class will pass the development fixture and fail here.
