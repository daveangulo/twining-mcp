# C28-H Oracle (HELD-OUT) — Distributed exchange across three vaults with a mid-run clean rebuild

**Sealed set.** Not used for development, debugging or tuning. Different names, wording, ordering, users, hosts, revisions and corpus tier from the development set. Unsealed only for the measured run.

**Surfaces asserted:** stored events/records (**E**), the in-force view (**V**), the seven exchange states (**D** — `written_locally`, `enqueued`, `arrived_remote`, `checked_and_admitted`, `materialised`, `placed_in_context`, `work_acknowledged`), the retained past (**H**) and action-qualification outcomes (**P**).

---

`tenant-saltmarsh`. Two teams: **team-harbor** owns `mod/freight/`; **team-kiln** owns `mod/tariff/`. Shared scope `mod/common/`.

- **hp-dalia** — harbor; read/write/publish in `mod/freight/`, read/write in `mod/common/`, read in `mod/tariff/`.
- **hp-esteban** — kiln; read/write in `mod/tariff/`, read in `mod/freight/` (**withdrawn mid-run by `WD-4`**).
- **hp-fenwick** — kiln; **read only** in `mod/tariff/` and `mod/common/`.
- Agents: **ag-harbor-claude** (Claude, for dalia), **ag-kiln-codex** (Codex, for esteban), **ag-freight-api** (API worker for dalia, **no direct memory tools**).
- Hosts: **host-delta** (machine-7, `vault-D`), **host-echo** (machine-8, `vault-E`, clock **−11 min**), **host-foxtrot** (machine-9, `vault-F`, empty install).
- Repositories: **P-FREIGHT-a19** (`freight-core` → relocated `freight-platform`; worktrees `wt-freight-trunk@host-delta`, `wt-freight-audit@host-echo`; submodule `sub-common`), **P-TARIFF-e44** (`tariff-rules`).

**Corpus.** Tier **T3 = 100,000** records seeded into `vault-D` (`mod/freight/` 61,000; `mod/tariff/` 34,000; `mod/common/` 5,000), with **14 markers `K-01..K-13` and `K-22`**. Pre-run manifest `BASELINE-9`. `K-07` (UTF-16LE + BOM + CRLF) and `K-08` (UTF-8, LF, no BOM) render identically and must never share a hash.

## 2. Order of operations (deliberately different from the development set)

1. **Q0** Provision three vaults and four credentials; seed `vault-D`; capture `BASELINE-9`.
2. **Q1** Fault traffic **first**: `X14` redelivered **five** times with an identical digest; `X03` admitted remotely with its acknowledgement lost (producer retries four more times); the id `X11` re-used with different bytes; `X31` (which depends on `X30`) delivered **before** `X30`.
3. **Q2** **Mid-run clean rebuild**: export `EXP-9` from `vault-D`, import into empty `vault-F`, rebuild derived indexes, then admit `vault-F` as a **live peer** that receives the rest of the run's traffic.
4. **Q3** Partition `vault-D` | `vault-E`. `X50` (dalia @host-delta, `routing=shortest-transit`, in force 14:20Z) and `X51` (dalia @**host-foxtrot**, `routing=lowest-cost`, in force **14:12Z** but wall-clock **14:31** local, arriving **after** `X50`) both supersede `K-03`. Meanwhile `X41` (fenwick, read-only, validly signed) tries to supersede `K-09`, and `X42` (validly signed by `ag-freight-api`, **author string claims hp-dalia**, targeting a `mod/tariff/` record) tries to write cross-team. `wt-freight-audit@host-echo` holds uncommitted edits throughout.
5. **Q4** Kill the writer process on host-delta **between the outbox write and the export/transfer step** for `X20`; full process restart; replay into the real consumer.
6. **Q5** `WD-4` withdraws esteban's `mod/freight/` read while `vault-E` is disconnected. `X60` — esteban's legitimate `mod/tariff/banding/` write. `X61` — dalia redacts `K-11`. `X62` — rotate `key-rebuild-foxtrot`. Relocate `P-FREIGHT-a19`. Reconnect `vault-E`, which still carries a pre-redaction `K-11` and a pre-withdrawal permission view.
7. **Q6** One real receiving turn on each of `run-cld-77/u4`, `run-cdx-81/u2`, `run-api-95/u1`, with emitted bytes captured.
8. **Q7** Measure and report.

## 3. What must be observable

### Replica histories (H)
- **B1.** `vault-D`, `vault-E` and `vault-F` each retain the full envelope of every marker plus every Q1–Q5 delta: origin URI + anchor, origin-bytes hash and algorithm, encoding, writer, witness, seen-at, in-force-at, scope, version. Asserted on named records.
- **B2.** `K-03`'s history holds `X50` and `X51` as admitted, and `X41` and `X42` as **refused with distinct reason codes**, on all three vaults.
- **B3.** `P-FREIGHT-a19` records survive the relocation bound to the same repository identity; `P-TARIFF-e44` never binds into it; the submodule and the two worktrees resolve as separate identities under one repository. No unsolicited commit, reset, checkout or stash touches `wt-freight-audit@host-echo`, whose uncommitted edits are intact at the end of the run.

### In-force view (V)
- **B4.** `V(K-03)` is **explicitly contested** over `{X50, X51}` with no resolution applied.
- **B5 (negative).** `V(K-03)` is identical when the arrival order of `X50`/`X51` is swapped and when host-echo's skew is flipped to **+11 min**. Nothing in the contested view derives from arrival order, wall clock or wording similarity.
- **B6 (negative).** Neither `X41` nor `X42` ever enters `V`, is cited in any `P` decision, or reappears as admitted after any rebuild. Their refusals are recorded **independently** — a single blanket refusal covering both is a failure, because each tests a different rule (insufficient rights vs. a credential whose scope cannot reach the record, with an author string that carries no authority).
- **B7 (negative).** `V(K-06)` is unchanged while `X30` is unadmitted; `X31` sits pending with `X30` named as its unmet prerequisite.
- **B8 (negative).** `K-07` and `K-08` keep distinct origin-bytes hashes and byte-exact content (UTF-16LE, BOM, CRLF preserved) in `E`, `V` and after both rebuilds. No rendered-output hash stands in for an origin-bytes hash.
- **B9 (negative).** `K-22` remains a **model inference** everywhere it appears. Its body reads as a settled, dated, owner-attributed decision and contains no hedge, no marker and no tell; nothing about its prose, headings, imperative mood, or any `actor` / `active` / `promoted_by` field may raise its class or let it close `TR-204`.
- **B10.** After `X61`, `K-11` is absent from `V` on all three vaults; the redaction record and the retained historical copy are visible in `H`; reconnecting the stale `vault-E` does not restore it.

### Exchange receipts (D)
- **B11.** All seven states are individually recorded and separately queryable for `X01..X14`, `X50`, `X51`, `X60`. `placed_in_context` and `work_acknowledged` are never satisfied by a storage acknowledgement.
- **B12 (negative).** `X14`: five deliveries, **one** semantic effect, zero duplicate admissions, `V` changed once.
- **B13.** `X03`: five producer attempts, one remote admission, reconciled to `work_acknowledged`, retry count observable.
- **B14 (negative).** The re-used `X11` id with different bytes is an explicit conflict; it is refused and **both** byte streams are retained under distinct hashes.
- **B15.** `X20` survives the outbox→export kill: nothing acknowledged is lost, partial progress is exposed rather than smoothed over, and replay into the real consumer is idempotent.
- **B16 (negative).** Nothing in the receipts, reports or diagnostics claims exactly-once network delivery, and nothing claims `K-11` has been erased from clones or backups; retention and clone limits are stated instead.
- **B17.** For `run-cld-77/u4`, `run-cdx-81/u2` and `run-api-95/u1`: candidate ids, **actual emitted bytes and hash**, receiving host/session/turn, budget decisions and exclusions are persisted, and *selected / emitted / delivered / unavailable* are distinguished. `run-api-95` declares its injection path and its blind spots; its integration is not inferred from the parent's configuration.

### Reconstruction (E, V, H, D)
- **B18.** `vault-F`, seeded only from `EXP-9` and an index rebuild, then fed the live Q3–Q5 traffic, ends equal to `vault-D` on: record id, payload digest, origin-bytes hash, encoding, evidence class, lifecycle state, relations, seen-at, in-force-at, writer.
- **B19 (negative).** Neither rebuild alters `K-22`'s evidence class, the refused status of `X41`/`X42`, the contested status of `K-03`, the redacted status of `K-11`, or `WD-4`'s effect.
- **B20 (negative).** No record marked admitted in `BASELINE-9` plus deltas is missing from `vault-F`, and `vault-F` holds nothing absent from the durable export.
- **B21.** A **second** export, from `vault-F` into another empty install, equals `vault-F` on the same fields — the rebuild is idempotent, not a one-way lossy projection.
- **B22.** Export + import + index rebuild at T3 is measured and reported; the 30-minute budget is asserted at the declared pilot corpus and reported as-measured at T3.

## 4. Positive controls
- **X60 / P6** — a legitimate in-scope write by the same principal whose other request is refused, proving the refusal path is discriminating rather than total.
- **P7 / K-09** — the authorised cross-scope mode returns the lesson that default scoped mode withholds, proving the scope filter is a filter and not a wall.
- **B11 on `X01..X14`** — the uneventful traffic completes all seven states, proving receipt assertions are not satisfied by a store that leaves everything pending.
